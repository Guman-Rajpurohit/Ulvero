## Built Around a Simple Idea

**The browser should work for you — not work against your privacy.**

</div>

Ulvero is a next-generation Android web browser focused on delivering a
fast, clean and privacy-oriented browsing experience.

It brings essential browsing controls, advertisement blocking, site
management and privacy features together in a minimalist interface.

---

# Minimal by Design

## Clean Home Screen

Ulvero starts with a focused home experience designed to get you from opening
the browser to the web as quickly as possible.

### Effortless Customization

Adapt the interface around the way you browse.

### Smart Shortcuts

Keep frequently visited websites within easy reach.

### Clutter-Free Interface

The interface stays intentionally minimal so that websites remain the focus.

### Distraction-Free Browsing

Reduce unnecessary visual elements and keep your attention on the content.

---

# Privacy Comes First

## Local-First Architecture

Ulvero is designed around processing and storing supported browsing data
locally on the device whenever possible.

The goal is simple:

> **Your browsing experience should remain under your control.**

### On-Device Storage

Supported browsing information can remain stored locally on your device.

### No Unnecessary Tracking

Ulvero is designed without unnecessary behavioral tracking.

### Local Processing

Supported browser and privacy operations are handled locally whenever possible.

### Session Protection

Browser sessions are designed with privacy and isolation in mind.

---

<div align="center">

## You're in Local Mode

<br>

Everything you browse is designed to stay under your control.

<br>

**No unnecessary servers.**

**No unnecessary tracking.**

**No unnecessary data collection.**

</div>

# Built-In Ad Blocking

## Browse Without the Clutter

Ulvero includes integrated advertisement and interruption blocking designed
to make websites cleaner and browsing more efficient.

### Pre-Roll Blocking

Block supported advertisements before video playback.

### Mid-Roll Blocking

Reduce supported advertisements appearing during video content.

### Banner Removal

Remove distracting advertising elements from supported websites.

### Pop-up Prevention

Prevent unwanted pop-ups and aggressive redirects.

### Reduced Ad Media

Prevent unnecessary advertising resources from loading where supported.

---

# A Cleaner Video Experience

Ulvero's blocking capabilities are designed to reduce interruptions while

# Advanced Privacy Controls

## Control Your Browsing Environment

Ulvero provides controls for users who want more visibility and control over
how websites behave.

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Ad Blocker | Block supported intrusive advertisements |
| JavaScript | Control JavaScript execution |
| Stealth Mode | Additional privacy-oriented controls |
| Pop-ups | Prevent unwanted pop-ups and redirects |
| Privacy Center | Centralize important browser controls |

---

# Site Management

## Everything You Need, Directly From the Browser

Ulvero is designed to make website-level controls accessible without forcing
users through complicated settings.

### Connection Security

View security information for the current website.

### Permissions

Review and manage supported website permissions, including:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Notifications
- Other supported permissions

### Cookies

Inspect and manage website cookies.

Supported actions include:

**Copy · Paste · Clear**

### Live Changes

Apply supported website controls and reload the page when required.

---

# Security at the Core

## Native Protection

Ulvero's privacy and protection features are designed as part of the browser
experience rather than relying entirely on external extensions.

### Privacy-Focused Architecture

Privacy is considered throughout the browsing experience.

### Secure Connections

Ulvero supports secure HTTPS connections when available.

### Native Controls

Important browsing controls are accessible directly from the browser.

### Local Resilience

Supported local functionality can continue without unnecessary dependence on
external services.

# Smart Link Bypass

<div align="center">

### Coming Soon

### Redirect Reduction

Attempt to remove unnecessary intermediary redirects.

### Direct Destination

Reach supported destination URLs with fewer steps.
